Fill Orders at show-me-source Automatically (one-time setup)

On a computer

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto it: SPBC Fill
  3. For each order below, click Fill at show-me-source ↗. In the tab that opens, apply your pricing code if needed, then click SPBC Fill in your bookmarks bar.

On your iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap
  2. In Safari, open any page, tap Share → Add Bookmark, name it SPBC Fill, tap Save.
  3. Tap the Bookmarks (book) icon → Edit → tap SPBC Fill. Delete the address, paste the copied code, tap Done.
  4. For each order below, tap Fill at show-me-source ↗. When their shop opens in Safari, apply your pricing code if needed, then tap the address bar, type SPBC Fill and tap the bookmark (or open Bookmarks and tap it).

It fills the cart, the customer's address and supplier@springfieldpbc.com, picks USPS Priority, ticks "No Show Me Source branding in the package" and asks them (in the notes) to put no invoice or prices in the box. It never ticks their agreement boxes or places the order: you check and pay.

Made your SPBC Fill bookmark before Nov 25? Copy and paste the code into it once more to get the newest version.
