Ordering

How do I order?

Pick at least 2 vials or 1 kit on the price list (any mix), fill in your shipping details and place the order. You get an order number and payment details right away, on screen and by email.

How do I pay?

Venmo, Chime or PayPal, sent as a personal payment (Venmo: Friends; PayPal: Friends and Family). Put your order number in the payment note. We confirm every payment by hand before shipping.

A payment sent as a purchase, goods and services or business payment is refunded minus the processor's fees, and the order stays unpaid until you pay again the right way.

Is BAC water included?

No. Bacteriostatic water is its own item on the price list. Add it if you want it.

Shipping

When will my order ship?

After your payment is confirmed. Orders ship on post-office days (not weekends or USPS holidays), and you get a tracking number by email.

How much is shipping, and where do you ship?

Standard or Priority, priced per order and shown before you place it. US addresses only.

How do I track my order?

Use the link in your shipping email, or Track Order with your order number. My Orders lists every order placed with your email.

What if my package is lost or late?

Once a package is with the carrier, delays and losses are the carrier's responsibility. If tracking stops moving, message us with your order number and we'll look at it with you.

Products

Do you have lab reports?

Yes. See Lab Reports. Products with a report also show "View Lab Report" on the price list.

What are these products for?

Laboratory research only. They are not for human or veterinary use. You must be 21 or older to order.

Policies

Can I get a refund or return something?

No. All sales are final. There are no refunds, returns or exchanges after payment is confirmed.

What do you do with my information?

We use it to fill and ship your order and nothing else. See Privacy.
