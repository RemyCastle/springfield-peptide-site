What We Collect

  • When you order: your name, email, shipping address, optional phone, what you ordered, and delivery notes.
  • When you pay: the payment notice from Venmo, Chime or PayPal (name, amount, note). We never see your card or bank details.
  • If you ask us to: your email for restock or price-drop alerts, contact-form messages, and reviews.
  • Site health: anonymous page-visit counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics, no cookies) and checkout error reports with no names, emails or addresses.

Why

To fill and ship your order, match your payment, send tracking, answer you, and keep the shop working. Nothing else.

Who Sees It

Only SPBC, plus the companies that do a job for us: our supplier ships your order to the address you gave (name, address, items), Resend sends our emails, and Cloudflare hosts the site. We never sell or rent your information.

How Long We Keep It

Orders and payment records are kept as business and tax records. Contact messages, alert sign-ups, review requests and similar extras are deleted automatically after a set time.

Your Choices

  • Every optional email has an "Email preferences / unsubscribe" link.
  • Want a copy of your data, or want it deleted? Contact us and we will do it (tax records we must keep are the only exception).
